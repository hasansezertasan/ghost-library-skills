# Contract audit

## Scope and identity

Target path/URL, revision or hashes, selected domains, artifacts reviewed, and exclusions.

## Findings

For each finding: priority, category, source location/case, concrete evidence, consumer impact, and smallest useful next action. Separate observed defects from questions requiring evidence or a behavior decision.

## Coverage and checks

Record parsed fixture files and case counts, traced rules, comparison/profile checks, broken artifact links, and any runner/conformance evidence reviewed. Do not equate number of cases with number of covered rules.

## Limitations

State what was not inspected or executed and which conclusions remain uncertain. With no findings, still state scope and evidence.
