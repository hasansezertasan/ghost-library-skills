# Contract extraction

## Source and attribution

Origin project, revision, license, and the attribution retained in the new contract. State whether expectations were derived from documentation, tests, observed behavior, or a combination.

## Compatibility scope

What the extracted contract preserves, what it deliberately excludes or changes, and which observed behaviors are classified as accidental quirks rather than requirements.
